FanBrella – Solar Powered Umbrella with Fan

Project Overview

FanBrella is a smart umbrella prototype designed to provide shade, cooling, and convenience during hot weather. It integrates a solar panel, rechargeable battery, fan, and LED lights.

Problem Statement

During hot weather, people often need both protection from sunlight and cooling. FanBrella aims to combine these features in a portable umbrella.

Objectives

- Provide shade and cooling.
- Use solar energy as a power source.
- Store energy in a rechargeable battery.
- Provide LED lighting when required.

Components Used

- Solar Panel
- DC Motor Fan
- Rechargeable 3.7V Battery
- LED Lights
- Switches and Connecting Wires
- Charge Controller
- DC-DC Converter
